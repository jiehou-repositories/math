Relations

Definition of relations

\(\forall\) sets \(X\) and \(Y\), a relation \(R\) between \(X\) and \(Y\) is a subset of \(X \times Y\).
\(\forall\ x \in X\) and \(\forall\ y \in Y\), \((x, y) \in R\) is denoted as \(x R y\).

Definition of equality relation

The equality relation \(=\) on a set \(S\) is defined by \(\{(x, x)\ |\ x \in S\}\).

Definition of equivalence relation

An equivalence relation \(\sim\) on a set \(S\) is one satisfying following properties:
  • Reflexive:
    \(\forall\ x \in S\), \(x \sim x\).
  • Symmetric:
    \(\forall\ x, y \in S\), if \(x \sim y\), then \(y \sim x\).
  • Transitive:
    \(\forall\ x, y, z \in S\), if \(x \sim y\) and \(y \sim z\), then \(x \sim z\).
