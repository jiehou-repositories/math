Polynomial

Definition

A function \(p : F \rightarrow F\) is called a polynomial with coefficients in \(F\) iff \(\exists\ a_{0}, \dots, a_{n} \in F\) and \(n \in \mathbb{Z}^{+}\) such that \(\forall\ x \in F\), \[ p(x) = \sum_{i = 0}^{n}a_{i}x^{i}. \] \(p\) has degree \(n\) iff \(a_{n} \ne 0\), denoted as \(\text{deg}\ p = n\).
\(\mathcal{P}(F)\) is the set of all polynomials with coefficients in \(F\).
\(\mathcal{P}_{n}(F)\) is the set of all polynomials with coefficients in \(F\) and degree at most \(n\).
